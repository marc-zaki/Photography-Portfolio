THE STORYTELLER

Framing emotions, freezing time.

I am Marc, a Photographer specializing in editorial, portrait, and cinematic view photography. My work revolves around capturing shadows, dramatic lighting, and deep narratives in everyday frames.

Every photograph is a page in a visual book. By manipulating depth of field, natural lighting, and high-contrast styling, I translate raw moments into cinematic frames that tell their own silent, powerful story.

Based in Cairo, I lead campaigns, collaborate on editorial lookbooks, and capture commercial spaces with a keen eye for geometry, human intimacy, and visual branding.
